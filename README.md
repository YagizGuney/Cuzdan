Wallet App 👛

This is a simple and user-friendly wallet application designed to help you keep track of your personal finances. You can easily record your daily transactions and manage your budget.

Features
- **Balance Overview:** Quickly check your total money.
- **Track Income & Expenses:** Easily add your earnings and spendings.
- **Transaction History:** View a list of your past records.
- **Clean UI:** A simple, minimal, and easy-to-use interface.
